# Appendix B: Errors

[Contents](README.md)

This appendix will list the SQLSTATE classes the driver reports, those
it makes of its own, such as SQLCODE -919 and SQLSTATE 08006 for a
connection the server's messages broke, and those the server sends, and
which of them end the connection. It is filled as the driver reports
each, from M1 to M7.
